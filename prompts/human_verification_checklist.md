# Human Verification Checklist

Use this checklist with the audit packet and review sheet.

## Source Grounding

- Does the source span preserve original wording?
- Does the span support the extracted claim?
- Is the span primary evidence, review summary, reassessment, or control
  evidence?

## Values And Units

- Are FE, NH3 yield, potential, current density, stability, and energy
  efficiency values copied correctly?
- Are units copied correctly?
- Is any normalization clearly documented?

## Nitrogen Source

- Is the nitrogen source N2, 15N2, NO3-, NO2-, NO, NOx, catalyst impurity, or
  unknown?
- Is the reaction family eNRR, LiNRR, NO3RR, NO2RR, NORR, mixed, or unclear?

## Validation Controls

- Is isotope validation explicitly reported?
- Are blank controls explicitly reported?
- Are contamination controls explicitly reported?
- Is NOx screening explicitly reported?
- Are review summaries kept separate from primary evidence?

## Reliability Label

- Does the label follow the A/B/C/D/Reject rubric?
- Should the reliability label be overridden?
- Is uncertainty documented in correction notes?

## Decision

- Accept the draft.
- Reject the draft.
- Include in gold with reliability override.
- Leave out until additional source checking is complete.
